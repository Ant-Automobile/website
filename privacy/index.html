In short

  • You do not need to give us any personal information to browse the site. Our servers keep only the technical records needed for security, and only for a short time.
  • The contact form does not store anything on our servers. It opens your own email app with the message ready; your details only reach us if you send that email.
  • We do not advertise, build profiles or sell or rent personal data.
  • Visit statistics, the only optional feature, run only if you consent, and you can withdraw consent at any time. We use no third-party measurement tool, only our own measurement system with a first-party cookie; it keeps its data on our server in Türkiye and never stores your IP address. The offline copy of our waiting page is always kept in your browser, but it never leaves your device.
  • For any request about your data, write to kvkk@antautomobile.org. We reply free of charge within one month.

02Scope and applicable law

This notice covers:

  • the English and Turkish pages under antautomobile.org, the 404 page and the waiting ("pit") page shown when one of our services is down or under maintenance;
  • the contact form on the site and emails you send to our @antautomobile.org addresses;
  • our correspondence about sponsorship, press, academic cooperation and similar topics;
  • internal services that require a login and are used only by our team members and invited partners.

We are based in Türkiye, so our processing is governed by the KVKK. Where our processing falls within the territorial scope of the GDPR (Article 3(2)), for example because you are in the European Economic Area and we monitor your use of the site with your consent, the GDPR applies as well. The Turkish version of our KVKK Aydınlatma Metni contains the information required under Turkish law.

05Our legitimate interests

Where we rely on legitimate interests, they are:

  • Security: protecting the site, our server and our visitors against attacks, abuse and unauthorised access. Short-lived server logs are the minimum needed for this.
  • Delivering the site well: showing it in a language you are likely to understand and keeping it available.
  • Communication: answering the questions and proposals you send us.
  • Legal claims: being able to show what was agreed if a dispute arises.

We have weighed these interests against your rights and keep the data involved to a minimum. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time.

06How the contact form works

The form on our site does not send your input to our server and does not store it. When you press "Send", your details open as a ready-made email in your own email app. They reach us only if you send that email, and your email provider (for example Gmail or Outlook) also processes it under its own policy. Before the email is prepared, you are asked to confirm that you have read this notice. That checkbox records that you were informed; it is not consent.

10Security

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk (Article 32 GDPR, Article 12 KVKK), including:

  • encrypted connections only (HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or later, HSTS);
  • our own mail server, so your emails are not held by a third-party email service;
  • a firewall, key-based remote access, automatic blocking of repeated failed logins and web applications that are only reachable through Cloudflare, via an encrypted tunnel or an encrypted connection open to Cloudflare only;
  • passwords stored only as irreversible hashes;
  • backups encrypted with AES-256 and stored only in encrypted form; only the team holds the key;
  • a strict Content Security Policy; our pages load no third-party measurement scripts, Cloudflare's injection of measurement scripts into pages is switched off, and our own measurement script loads only with your consent;
  • no IP addresses in analytics records; the client ID is random and not linked to your identity, and only team members with personal accounts can see analytics data;
  • regular updates, continuous monitoring and automatic outage alerts;
  • access limited to the team members who need it, with personal accounts that are removed when someone leaves.

If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the competent authority without undue delay and within 72 hours where required, and inform you without undue delay where the risk is high.

11Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights. The KVKK gives you equivalent rights under its Article 11.

  • Access (Art. 15): find out whether we process your data and get a copy.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): have your data deleted, for example when we no longer need it.
  • Restriction (Art. 18): ask us to limit how we use your data while a question is being resolved.
  • Data portability (Art. 20): receive data you gave us, based on consent or a contract, in a common machine-readable format.
  • Objection (Art. 21): object at any time, on grounds relating to your situation, to processing based on legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need the data for legal claims.
  • Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)): at any time, with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.
  • Notification of recipients (Art. 19): have corrections or deletions passed on to the recipients of your data.

12How to exercise your rights

Write to kvkk@antautomobile.org, ideally from the email address you used to contact us before. Tell us which right you want to exercise and include enough detail for us to find your data. We may ask for additional information to confirm your identity, so that we do not disclose your data to someone else.

We reply free of charge within one month of receiving your request. If a request is complex, we may extend this by up to two further months and will tell you why within the first month. Requests under Turkish law are answered within 30 days, as set out in the Turkish notice.

13Complaints

If you think we have not handled your data properly, please tell us first so we can try to fix it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place (Art. 77 GDPR). A list of EU authorities is available from the European Data Protection Board. In Türkiye, the competent authority is the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu.

14Is providing data required?

You are not required by law or contract to give us personal data. To reply to you, we need your name, email address and message; the organisation field is optional. If you do not provide them, we simply cannot answer.

15Automated decisions

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR). Choosing the site language from your country is only a convenience and can always be changed with the language button. The pseudonymous client ID used for analytics only lets us count visits in aggregate (for example new and returning visitors and sessions); it is not used to identify you, to make decisions about you or to build advertising profiles.

16Children

The site is open to everyone but is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are under 16, please talk to a parent or guardian before using the contact form or writing to us. If you believe a child has sent us personal data without their parent's knowledge, write to kvkk@antautomobile.org and we will delete it.

17Special categories of data

We do not collect special categories of personal data (Art. 9 GDPR, Art. 6 KVKK), such as data about health, religion, political opinions, ethnic origin, biometric or genetic data, or criminal convictions. Please do not send us such information. If you do, and it is not needed to answer you, we delete it.

18Links and social media

Our site only links to our LinkedIn, Instagram and X accounts; no content, buttons or tracking scripts from these platforms are embedded in our pages. They do not learn through us that you visited our site unless you click a link. Once you do, the privacy policy of the site you visit applies. When you interact with our accounts on those platforms, the platform is a separate controller for its own processing.

19Cookies

Without your consent we only use cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary, including the offline copy of our waiting page (functional), which stays on your device. Analytics runs only if you consent; in that case our server stores the first-party ant_a cookie with a pseudonymous client ID in your browser (13 months, renewed on each visit). The name, purpose and lifetime of everything we use is listed in our Cookie Policy.

20Changes

We update this notice when our processing or the law changes. The current version is always on this page, with its effective date and version number at the top. If a change affects optional cookies, we ask for your choice again.
