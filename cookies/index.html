02Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. Your browser sends it back with later requests, so the site can, for example, remember the language you chose.

Other technologies can serve the same purpose. When we say "cookies" in this policy, we include them:

  • Local storage (localStorage): small records kept in your browser that are not sent to the site automatically. We keep your theme choice here, for example.
  • Service worker and cache (Cache Storage): a small program and copies of pages that a site stores in your browser so it can show some pages without an internet connection.
  • Scripts (JavaScript): code running in the page that can send information to a server. Our own measurement script for visit statistics reads information from your browser and lets our server store a cookie, so it depends on your consent. Our pages load no third-party measurement scripts.

03Types of cookies

By lifetime
Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies stay until they expire or you delete them.
By origin
First-party cookies are set by antautomobile.org itself. Third-party cookies are set by another domain. The only third-party cookies that may appear on our site are Cloudflare security cookies, and only when Cloudflare deems them necessary.
By purpose
Strictly necessary, functional, analytics and marketing. Each category we use is described below.

To improve the site we want to know which pages are visited, where visitors come from and how they move through the site. We do not use a third-party analytics service for this, only our own measurement system: the measurement script in the page sends each interaction, such as a page view, scroll or click, as an event to our own server only, together with the pseudonymous client ID in the first-party ant_a cookie set by our server. The script loads only if you consent; otherwise it is never sent to your browser, no cookie is stored and no measurement data reaches our server.

09How we ask for consent

  • On your first visit a banner appears at the bottom of the screen. "Reject all" and "Accept all" have the same size and look; rejecting is as easy as accepting.
  • "Reject all" switches off analytics. The strictly necessary and functional technologies stay on, because the site and its offline waiting page need them.
  • "Manage preferences" shows every category. Strictly necessary and functional are marked "Always on"; analytics, the only optional category, can be switched on or off and is not pre-ticked.
  • Analytics does not run until you switch it on. The banner does not block the site; you can use it without choosing.
  • Continuing to use the site, scrolling or ignoring the banner does not count as consent.
  • Your choice is kept for 180 days. After that, or when this policy changes significantly, we ask again.
  • To be able to prove consent, we record your choice, its date, the policy version and a random consent ID. We do not add your IP address or browser details to this record, and we keep it for 3 years.

10Changing your choice and withdrawing consent

Use the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page, or the button at the top of this page, to change your choice at any time. What this means for each category:

  • Analytics: when you switch it off, our measurement script is not loaded again, the ant_a cookie holding the client ID (and the ant_a_cid, ant_a_s local records, if left over from the previous version) is deleted from your browser at once, and the open page reloads so that the script leaves memory.
  • Functional: cannot be switched off here, because it is strictly necessary. If you want to remove it, clear this site's data in your browser (see section 11): the service worker is unregistered and the copy of the waiting page is deleted. It is registered again on your next visit.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

12International transfers

Our analytics data is stored only on our own server in Türkiye; we do not use Cloudflare's measurement or analytics services. Like every other request to the site, it reaches our server through Cloudflare's network over an encrypted connection; the safeguards for serving the site through Cloudflare are described in section 8 of our Privacy Notice. Strictly necessary and functional cookies and records stay on your device.
