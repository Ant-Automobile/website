Parts alone are not a car.

ANT Automobile is a student team that brings mechanics, electronics and software to the same table. We design every part of our car ourselves and back every decision with measurement.

Our first car is a two-seat, four-wheel electric city car. With it, we will race in the Elektromobil category of the TEKNOFEST 2027 Efficiency Challenge.

Assembly0%

Our vision

To become an engineering community in Türkiye that creates its own knowledge of electric mobility, designs its own vehicles and passes that knowledge on to the next generation.

Our mission

To build a real car, from start to finish, with our own hands.

  1. Putting efficiency at the heart of design

    Designing cars that know the energy cost of every decision and go further on fewer resources.

  2. Developing our own technology

    Designing the car's control and battery electronics within the team instead of leaning on off-the-shelf solutions.

  3. Making knowledge last

    Documenting everything we learn, so the next team starts where we left off, not from scratch.

Four principles that guide us

  • Honest measurement

    We never claim what we have not measured. We always keep estimates and evidence apart.

  • Safety first

    We don't add safety later; we build it in from the very first line of the design.

  • Precision

    We know small details lead to big outcomes. Every part we choose has a reason.

  • Building together

    A car emerges when different disciplines work toward the same goal. We share knowledge rather than keep it.

We work on every layer that makes up a car.
